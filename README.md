### A bit about me

I'm a product-minded developer who's happiest when I'm building something, solo, or with a team. I'm big into **open source**, and I specialize in **full stack development**, mainly across the **MERN/Next.js** stack.

Lately I've been deep into **agentic systems**, **AI agents**, and **no-code tools**, figuring out how to make software that thinks a bit for itself. I also dabble in **mobile development** on the side.

At the core, I'm solution-oriented: I like finding real problems and actually solving them, one project at a time.

-  Currently exploring multi-agent AI systems & RAG pipelines
-  Always learning something new in the agentic AI space
-  Open to collaborating on full stack, AI, or anything genuinely interesting
